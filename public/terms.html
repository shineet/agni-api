What Agni does

Agni records what you eat, reads what Apple Health knows about your movement, sleep and recovery, and reads them together: a daily food budget, a movement goal, and plain-language guidance about what matters now. It works out food figures from photographs, from a bundled table of dishes, from public nutrition databases and, when you allow it, from an AI model.

The figures are estimates

This is the most important thing to understand about the app, and Agni says so on the screen as well as here.

  • A photograph cannot measure. It cannot see the oil in a curry, how much ghee went on a roti, or what is underneath what. Agni makes an informed estimate from what is visible and tells you when it is unsure. You can review and adjust estimates.
  • The dish table is a reference, not your kitchen.
  • Apple Health figures are only as good as what recorded them.
  • The budget and the guidance are built on top of estimates and inherit every uncertainty underneath them.

Agni is designed to be useful for noticing patterns over weeks. It is not designed to be exact on any single plate.

Your health is your responsibility

You decide what to eat and how to train. Nothing Agni shows is an instruction. If something it tells you contradicts your doctor, your doctor is right. Where you set a weight goal, the app applies general safety floors, but those floors know nothing about you specifically and are not a substitute for professional advice.

Agni Free includes manual logging, quick add, food search, barcode scanning, calories and macros, water, Apple Health, weight and goals, history, workout records and the Apple Watch basics. Free also includes a one-time sample of AI photo analyses and AI food searches for each copy of the app.

Agni Pro adds “For you now”, recovery and movement assessments, workout notes, and AI analysis of meal photos, nutrition labels and food searches, within the allowances below.

In Agni 1.0 (build 180) and later, AI analysis only runs after you allow it in the app. Switching it off in Settings > Agni Pro makes Agni ask again before each AI use.

AI allowances and limits

AI analysis costs real money each time it runs, so Pro comes with allowances, not unlimited use.

The allowances. Each month Pro includes:

  • 100 photo or nutrition-label analyses, up to 25 a day
  • 60 AI food searches, dish descriptions and ingredient breakdowns
  • 5 ingredient web lookups

What counts as one. One photo analysis is one photo or nutrition label you send for an estimate. It includes everything Agni does to answer it: splitting a busy plate into parts, identifying a drink, and automatic retries. Sending it again with a correction counts as a new analysis. One AI search is one search (including the refinements you type while shaping it), one dish description, or one ingredient breakdown. One web lookup is one ingredient Agni looks up on the web because no saved answer exists; a saved answer does not count.

During a free trial the allowance is 25 analyses, 25 searches and 3 ingredient lookups for the trial period. Allowances reset every month on your subscription date, on the annual plan too. Unused allowance does not carry over. Settings > Agni Pro shows what is left and when it resets.

AI access can stop before you reach those numbers. The numbers are what Pro includes in a period, not a promise that every one will always be available. There are four separate reasons it can stop early:

1. Daily photo limit. At most 25 photo or label analyses in one day. The day is counted in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC), so more become available at midnight UTC.

2. A cost safeguard. Each period also has a limit on what your AI use costs to run. Analyses do not all cost the same: a plate with many dishes, a long answer, or a lookup that searches the web costs more than a simple one. The safeguard is set above what typical use of the allowances costs, but unusually heavy use can reach it before the counts are used. If it stops AI analysis while you still have allowance left, the app tells you and the stop is recorded. Write to me and I will review it; in Agni 1.0 (build 180) and later, include the Support ID shown in Settings > Agni Pro.

3. Agni's emergency spending maximum. Agni sets a ceiling on what the whole service spends on AI each day. As spending approaches a lower daily level, AI analysis pauses until the next day for Free users and then for trials. Subscribers are paused only if the whole service reaches its emergency daily maximum.

4. Outages and overload. If the AI provider is down or overloaded, or requests arrive very fast, AI analysis may be slowed or unavailable for a while. This is about the service, not anything you used up.

Whenever AI analysis is unavailable, everything else in Agni keeps working: logging by hand, quick add, food search, barcode scanning, your budget, Apple Health, your history and the Watch app.

If you think a limit has stopped you unfairly, or AI access has been unavailable for a long stretch, write to me.
