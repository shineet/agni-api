Common questions

The calories for a photo look wrong

A photo estimate is a careful guess from what the camera can see, and it cannot see oil, sugar or what is under the rice. Tap any dish on the review screen to change its portion, swap it for a different food, or correct the figures before you save. If Agni misread the photo entirely, use Tell Agni what it is and take it again with a short hint.

Agni only picked up part of my table

Agni estimates the plate in front of you, not everything in the frame. For a shared spread, photograph your own plate, or add the rest by hand.

My steps, workouts or burned energy do not show

Agni reads these from Apple Health and only with your permission. Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps (or Privacy > Apps), choose Agni, and turn on the categories you want it to read.

How do I delete a meal, or everything?

Open the meal in your history and tap Delete meal. That also removes what Agni wrote to Apple Health for it. Your food and health data live only on your iPhone, so deleting Agni deletes all of it (meals already written to Apple Health stay there until you remove them in the Health app). For anything kept on the server (see the Privacy Policy), email me and I will delete it.

Is Agni medical advice?

No. Agni is a tracking and awareness tool. Its numbers are estimates and its coach is not a doctor or dietitian. If you have a medical condition, an eating disorder, or are pregnant, talk to a professional before changing how you eat or exercise.

Purchases and refunds

Anything bought in Agni is sold through the App Store, so Apple handles payment, cancellations and refunds. To cancel a subscription, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To ask for a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
