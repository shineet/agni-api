A Brighter You

Privacy Policy · Last updated 1 October 2026

Your food and your health data stay on your phone.

Agni has no accounts and no sign-in. It does not know your name. This page describes exactly what the app does with information, what leaves your device and what does not.

The short version. Your meals, your weight, your workouts and everything Agni reads from Apple Health are stored on your iPhone and are never uploaded. When you photograph a meal, that single photograph is sent to be analysed and is discarded as soon as the answer comes back. It is never stored on a server. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics trackers and nothing is ever sold.

Who this is

Agni is made by Shine Thankappan, an independent developer. There is no company behind it and no team. If you write to me, I read it myself.

What stays on your iPhone, always

All of the following is held in Agni's own storage on your device. None of it is uploaded to me, and I have no way to see it:

  • Every meal you log, its dishes, calories, macros and the small photo thumbnail Agni keeps so you can recognise your own meals in your history
  • Everything Agni reads from Apple Health: steps, energy burned, heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep and workouts
  • Your body details, weight, goals and targets
  • Your settings, including your personal Anthropic API key if you choose to add one, which is held in the iPhone Keychain
  • Everything the coach says to you, which is worked out on your phone and never sent anywhere

If you delete Agni, all of it goes with the app.

Apple Health

Agni asks permission before reading anything, and you choose what to share. It reads steps, energy, heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep and workouts in order to show what you have burned against what you have eaten. It writes the meals you log back to Health as dietary energy and macros, so other apps you use can see them.

Health data is read on your device and used on your device. It is never uploaded, never sent to any third party, and never used for advertising. You can withdraw any permission at any time in the Health app, under Sharing.

What the install identifier is, and is not

It is a random value with no meaning. It is not your Apple ID, not your device's advertising identifier, not your phone number and not derived from anything about you. Its only job is counting free estimates so the shared service is not drained by one copy of the app. It cannot be traced back to who you are, because I never learn who you are.

The companies involved

Three services make Agni work. None of them receives your health data.

  • Anthropic analyses meal photographs and answers food questions. Photographs reach them without any identifier attached. Their handling of API data is set out in their own privacy policy and commercial terms.
  • Vercel runs Agni's server, which passes photographs through and keeps the dish table.
  • Supabase holds that dish table, the usage counts and, if you asked for a beta invitation, your email address.

USDA FoodData Central and FatSecret answer food lookups. They receive a food name and nothing else.

If you joined the beta

If you entered your email address to ask for a TestFlight invitation, that address and your name are kept so the invitation can be sent and so I can answer you. Your IP address is kept briefly, only to stop the form being abused, and is never shown to anybody. Ask me and I will delete all of it.

Using your own API key

If you add your own Anthropic API key in Settings, Agni talks to Anthropic directly from your phone and none of my servers are involved at all. The key is held in your iPhone's Keychain, is never transmitted to me, and I cannot read it.

Children

Agni is not designed for or directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect anything from them.

What I do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of anything, to anyone, ever
  • No profile of you, because there is no account to attach one to

Your rights, and how to use them

Because Agni holds your food and health data on your own device, you already control almost all of it. Deleting a meal deletes it. Deleting the app deletes everything it held.

For the small amount kept on the server, write to me and I will delete it. Depending on where you live you may have rights under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act to see, correct, export or delete what is held about you, and to complain to your data protection authority. Ask and I will do it; I do not require a reason.

Changes to this policy

If this changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes and the app will say so. I will not quietly broaden what Agni does with your information.
